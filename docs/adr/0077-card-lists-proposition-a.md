# 77. Card lists: a tidy boxed list, shared tags said once, math drawn (#129, #128)

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Amended by: ADR 0079 (the row look: no box, the deck page's cards grouped by their other tags with no pills on the rows, a due word at each row's end, All Cards' status off the second line; the shared tags, the header above the list and the drawn math stay)
- Refers to: issues #129 and #128; Phase 3b brief, slice 3; mockup `docs/briefs/mockups/deck-page-mockups.html`, proposition A (chosen by the maintainer); ADR 0013 (the deck page's `GtkListView`), 0042 (card rows and tag pills), 0044 (All Cards), 0049 (time bounds), 0073 (a row change costs no walk of the list), 0076 (repaint margin); GTK 4.24 and Pango 1.58 sources cited below

## Context

The deck page and All Cards read like a spreadsheet: the same four tag pills on every row of a deck whose cards all share them, three lines per row on All Cards, a plain list on a white view, and a formula's LaTeX source ("Mesure \sigma-finie") where the card shows "σ". The maintainer chose proposition A of the mockups: the same page, tidied. The brief asks for one shared row widget for both lists, in a boxed-list look inside a `GtkListView` so 10,000 cards stay smooth; two-line rows; the tags every listed card shares said once above the list and only the other tags on each row, with "+N"; on All Cards, the deck name and due date on the second line, and shared tags above the list only when every card listed has them; and the formulas of the summaries drawn at row size on one line, broken LaTeX falling back to its text.

## Decision

**One row, two lines.** `CardRow` stays the row of both lists (ADR 0042, 0044). Its first line is the front, its second the back. On All Cards the second line starts with the status and the deck, "Due in 3 days · Maths ·", then the back; on a narrow window (the 400 sp breakpoint the rows already follow, `compact`) it is the status and the deck only, which then fit at 360 px in English and French. The status comes before the deck so that a long deck name, not the status, ends in "…"; the deck filter names the deck anyway. The card's own tags are pills at the row's end, vertically centred, as in the mockup, with "+N" past three (one on a narrow row). A suspended card keeps its pause icon and also has its front dimmed, as the mockup's suspended row.

**Shared tags.** `card_row::text::shared_tags` gives the tags every card of the list has, in the first card's order, by tag id, and none for a list of fewer than two cards (with one card, "on every card" would say nothing). `other_tags` gives a row's pills without them. The same rule serves both pages: on a deck page the list is the deck; on All Cards it is the cards listed after the search and filters, which share a tag mostly after filtering by deck or tag, so most of the time each row shows its own tags there. The page holds the shared tags as two properties, `shared-tags` (ids, which the rows follow through `card_row::factory`, so every row on screen updates when they change) and `shared-tag-names` (shown by the header). Reading the items costs time in their total number of tags (sets of ids, `card_row::text::SharedTags`): about 3.5 ms for 10,000 cards that all share four tags (debug build), and less when nothing is shared, since the walk reads one item at a time and stops once no tag is left. It runs once the main loop is idle after any number of changes (`SharedTagsQueue`, at `G_PRIORITY_HIGH_IDLE`, which comes before GTK's layout and drawing, so no frame shows the old tags): a thousand single-row changes of All Cards still cost no walk of the list each (ADR 0073's test).

**The header sits above the list, not in it.** "On every card:" and the shared tags, wrapping, are a `CardListHeader` above the list's scrolled window, in the same 600 px clamp. A `GtkListView` header (a `header-factory` over the model's one section) was tried first, since it scrolls with the rows as in the mockup, and abandoned: when a page focuses its first row before the list is laid out (every time a deck page opens), `gtk_list_base_scroll_to_item` anchors that row, not yet allocated, at the top of the view (gtk/gtklistbase.c, `gtk_list_view_get_allocation` places an unallocated item after the last allocated tile, here at 0), so the header ended above the view; under Xvfb the view was scrolled by exactly the header's 42 px every time. The header is hidden while no tag is shared.

**The boxed list.** libadwaita's `.boxed-list` styles only a `GtkListBox` (`list.boxed-list > row:first-child`, `_lists.scss`), and a list box makes every row, which 10,000 cards cannot afford (ADR 0013). So the list view gets `card-list` and its rows draw their part of the box (`style.css`): libadwaita's card colours (`--card-bg-color`, `--card-fg-color`), its `--card-shade-color` for the line under each row and the sides (`--border-color` with high contrast), 12 px round corners and outer spacing on `row:first-child` and `row:last-child`, the boxed list's 3 % and 8 % hover and press shades, and the window's background behind (the scrolled window lost its `view` class). The first and last children are the first and last rows that exist, not the list's; but a list view keeps two rows alive past each end of what it shows (`GTK_LIST_VIEW_EXTRA_ITEMS`, gtk/gtklistview.c) and keeps its rows in the list's order (gtk/gtklistitemmanager.c), so those rows are on screen only when they are the list's own first and last. When a row off screen becomes the first or last and gains its margin, the list keeps the rows on screen in place (it scrolls by an anchor row); only the scroll bar's estimate moves a little.

**Math in the rows (#128).** `content::summary` (core, pure) makes the same one line as `plain_summary`, but as text and formula pieces, plus a plain text with each formula as its source, for screen readers. The row's accessible label is nearly unchanged: a formula that starts past the 200-character cut is now left out (before, its first characters were said), and one that starts just before it is said whole, so a label can be up to 1 KiB longer. A new widget, `render::MathLine`, replaces the two labels: one Pango layout with the object replacement character and an `AttrShape` per formula, as `MathParagraph` does, but ellipsised at the end and never wrapped. The formulas are drawn by the line itself into cairo nodes with `RaTeX`, not as child widgets, so a recycled row's bind only replaces a string. A formula Pango ellipsised away is not drawn: Pango replaces the cut text by one run whose item covers it and is flagged `PANGO_ANALYSIS_FLAG_IS_ELLIPSIS` (pango/ellipsize.c, `shape_ellipsis`). Formulas are drawn at the line's font × 1.21 as everywhere in the app, shrunk to at most 1.6 ems tall so rows keep about one line's height (a fraction is shrunk a little, a matrix a lot); one wider than the whole line is cut and followed by "…". Invalid LaTeX, a formula over the renderer's limits, a formula longer than 1 KiB, and formulas past 32 in one line are their source, as plain text; a formula that starts past the summary's 200 characters is left out, like the rest of the text there. The line's CSS name is `label`, so libadwaita's `row label.subtitle` styles the back as it styled the label. Each snapshot marks its area grown by 2 px for repainting (`ink_box::mark_damage`, ADR 0076), since the line draws its own text and recycled rows replace text in place.

Alternatives not taken:

- **Proposition B or C**: the maintainer chose A.
- **Shared tags from the rows on screen only**: they would change while scrolling.
- **Rows as `AdwActionRow`s in a list box**: the `.boxed-list` style for free, but every row made at once.
- **One `MathParagraph` per summary**: a scroller and a `MathView` per formula per bind, and no ellipsis.
- **Formulas at the text's own size (no 1.21)**: they look small beside the text, and differ from the card.

## Consequences

- A deck whose cards share tags shows them once, and each row only what sets the card apart; at 360 px the shared tags wrap above the list.
- On All Cards without a filter, rows show their own tags as before.
- The back no longer shows on All Cards rows on a narrow window; it is still in the row's accessible label and in the card's editor and information.
- Binding a row now lays out its formulas: binding one row to 4,000 cards, each with a formula of its own, took about 380 ms of CPU in a debug build, against 100 ms before (the test's bound is 2 s). Opening a deck of 10,000 cards that all share four tags took about 0.2 s to build the page and 0.5 s to its first frame in a debug build under Xvfb; the real app opened the 10,000-card deck page and All Cards within half a second.
- `fill_scratch_db --probability` adds the mockup's deck (17 cards, four shared tags, some with two more, math on both sides, one suspended) for trying this out.
- Not verified by eye: the look in the maintainer's own session (fonts, scaling, the GL renderer), the focus ring on the first and last rows, and how Orca reads the header.
